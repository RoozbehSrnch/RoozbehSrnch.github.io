About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in blockchain security and cryptography at the COSIC research group of KU Leuven. I received my Ph.D. in Blockchain Security from KU Leuven in September 2025 and hold an M.Sc. in Secure Communications and Cryptography from Sharif University of Technology and a B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Tehran. My research focuses on the security analysis of permissionless blockchains and the incentive structures that ensure their stability.

I investigate consensus mechanisms for both Proof‑of‑Work and Proof‑of‑Stake systems and study attack vectors such as selfish mining and selfish proposing. By applying cryptographic tools, I work towards scalable, fair and widely deployable decentralized systems.

Research Interests

  • Security analysis of blockchain consensus mechanisms and incentive structures
  • Time‑averaged analysis of selfish mining attacks in Bitcoin
  • Selfish proposing and rational mining behaviour in Proof‑of‑Stake protocols
  • Secure and fair reward distribution in permissionless blockchains
  • Cryptographic protocols for scalability and privacy
