Cryptography, Incentive Analysis, and Security in Permissionless Blockchains

About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the COSIC research group of KU Leuven working on the security and incentive design of permissionless blockchains. I obtained my Ph.D. in Blockchain Security from KU Leuven under the supervision of Bart Preneel. My thesis, Incentive Analysis in Permissionless Blockchains, examines how reward mechanisms and game‑theoretic behaviour shape the robustness of blockchain systems. My curriculum vitae and PhD thesis provide further details on my background and contributions.

My research combines cryptographic techniques with economic models to study incentives, consensus and security in decentralised systems. I investigate how reward mechanisms and strategic behaviour impact the robustness of permissionless blockchains and financial markets, and explore mitigation strategies that lead to secure, fair and scalable decentralised infrastructures.

Research Interests

  • Incentive and game‑theoretic analysis of permissionless blockchains and decentralised finance
  • Security analysis of consensus protocols, mining/proposing behaviour and reward mechanisms
  • Design of cryptographic protocols and privacy‑enhancing technologies for scalable blockchain systems
  • Applications of decentralised ledgers in energy trading, auctions and digital markets
