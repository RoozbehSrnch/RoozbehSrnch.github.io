About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the COSIC research group of KU Leuven working on the security and incentive design of permissionless blockchains. I obtained my Ph.D. in Blockchain Security from KU Leuven under the supervision of Bart Preneel. My thesis, Incentive Analysis in Permissionless Blockchains, examines how reward mechanisms and game‑theoretic behaviour shape the robustness of blockchain systems.

My research explores consensus mechanisms in both Proof‑of‑Work and Proof‑of‑Stake networks and studies attacks such as selfish mining, selfish proposing and commitment attacks. By combining cryptographic techniques with economic models, I aim to build secure, fair and scalable decentralised systems.

Research Interests

  • Incentive analysis of permissionless blockchains
  • Security and game‑theoretic analysis of blockchains & financial systems
  • Cryptography and cryptographic protocols
